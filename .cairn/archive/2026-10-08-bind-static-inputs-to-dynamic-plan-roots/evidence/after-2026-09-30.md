## Boundaries

A producer's plan content and root build can be wrong or nondeterministic.
Request identity and an observed root association do not prove correctness
or release eligibility. The persisted artifact provenance is **UNSIGNED**,
verified by canonical BLAKE3 digest only. Signed PathInfo covers its own
store metadata and references, not the attestation digest. No broad
formatter, project-wide build, installed Cairn `current` policy, or
isolated spec sync/archive is claimed by these dynamic-slice checks.
