## What recording the identities exposed

1. **The pinned revision is unpublished.** `git fetch --depth=1 git@github.com:OnixResearch/trellis.git d91188e495d65bca87551b7a06330efb2c909edf` into a throwaway repository exits 128 with `fatal: remote error: upload-pack: not our ref d91188e495d65bca87551b7a06330efb2c909edf`. The revision is not an ancestor of `origin/main`, and no remote-tracking branch contains it. It exists only on the owner's local `main`.
2. **The vendored source cannot be traced to a published revision.** At `origin/main` and at the two published fenced-attempt branch tips, only `LICENSE` matches the pin; `verified.rs`, `verified_positive.rs` and `verified_negative.rs` are absent at all three. The vendored bytes are intact and digest-bound, so Mantle builds are unaffected. What fails is the claim that they come from `https://github.com/OnixResearch/trellis` at that revision, which a fresh clone cannot reproduce.
3. **The published acceptance covers a different model.** The two spec texts differ only by 8 added lines at the pin, which introduce "the additional `fenced_attempt_admission::verified` kernel" over "seven phases and seven report kinds" and state "The earlier model and its evidence remain separate". The published archive's verification record describes "seven closed phases and six closed report kinds". The accepted archive (2026-09-03) therefore does not show the consumed kernel to be accepted.
4. **The producer bundle covers part of the requirement set.** The kernel's record cites 5 of the 8 requirement IDs: `fenced-attempt-admission.completion-linkage`, `fenced-attempt-admission.event-idempotence`, `fenced-attempt-admission.fence-safety`, `fenced-attempt-admission.rejection-preservation`, `fenced-attempt-admission.terminal-closure`. It does not cite `fenced-attempt-admission.boundary`, `fenced-attempt-admission.evidence`, `fenced-attempt-admission.refinement`.

## What this does not establish

No fresh Verus, Tracey, Cargo or Nix run, no acceptance of the consumed kernel, no proof that Mantle's projection
matches it (that is I8 and V3 to V5), no Kamacite envelope or Valence property receipt (I6), no runtime authority, no
release eligibility. The remote-availability result holds at the observation time only.

## What would resolve it

The Trellis owner publishes the pinned revision, or its successor, to a ref on the remote together with the kernel's
acceptance; Mantle then re-imports from that published revision with `scripts/import-trellis-fenced-attempt.py`
and records the new revision. The Trellis checkout has 572 uncommitted paths and an unpushed local `main`, so
this record does not publish, merge or change anything there.
